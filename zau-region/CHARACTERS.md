# Zau — Characters

Companion to [`STORY.md`](STORY.md). That file is the plot machinery; this one is the people running it. A character existing here doesn't mean they need a coded NPC yet — it means that when they do get a sprite, a position on a map, and dialogue, this is who they are, not a blank slot with a name on it.

Every entry has a **role** (why they exist in the plot), a **voice** (how their dialogue reads), and **what they say when** (how their lines change as the story advances — the thing the dialogue engine needs to key on). Pronouns are stated where the character has them established; where they aren't, dialogue uses they/them.

---

## Main cast

### The player
Nameless until the player names them; appearance is the player's. Speaks only through choices. Everyone else talks *to* them, which is why the cast has to be good.

### Alma — the player's mother
Runs a repair shop on the ground floor of their building, keeping the old underpass transit cars running — the ones Meridian has no interest in because there's no profit in them. Good at it, proud of it, pointedly doesn't work for the company. Old enough to remember the storms being milder.
- **Role**: home base. The reason the player's Bag is a repaired one. The phone call at the very end.
- **Voice**: direct, dry, no long goodbyes. Cares through logistics: "you got your bag, you got your balls, you got a way to call me."
- **Says when**: Act 1 — sends the player off. After Badge 1 — "the storm took the roof off the depot." After the Key Stone — she knew Elena Voss, a little; "she fixed things too. Then she was gone." Ending — the call.

### Priya — the friend who's coming too
Grew up two floors down. Gets a starter from Mabosso the same day, isn't chasing badges — she wants to *study* Pokémon, and is only in the Wild Zone because it's the only way anyone gets anywhere in Zau. Shows up at milestones rather than at the player's shoulder.
- **Role**: the observer. She notices what officials won't admit: the storms come from under, the Absol before every storm, the plants that grow wrong. Her samples and Prism's data are half the evidence that surfaces at the end. In the Underlight she's at the rig controls with Elena.
- **Voice**: curious, talks fast when excited, undercuts herself with jokes. Good listener when it isn't about Pokémon.
- **Says when**: end of the Trail — notes on the trainers. Harbor Lighthouse — "they come from under." Greenline — the Absol notes; "that was a *message*." Signal — decoding the pulse with Prism. Underlight — "I've got the controls. Go."

### Dario Voss — the rival
Loud about his confidence in a way that's clearly compensating. Checks his team's items three times before every battle — competence as a costume for anxiety. Takes old electronics apart when stressed, like his mother did. Not cruel: waits for the player to heal before a rematch, compliments a good play mid-loss, checks in sideways after the player's had a rough time. His flaw is that he doesn't know how to want something quietly.
- **Role**: the mirror. Meridian's offer to him is the only thing he's ever wanted — someone treating a Voss as worth investing in — and it's real, which is what makes turning it down cost something.
- **Voice**: talks in challenges even when he doesn't mean them ("bet your Sprigatito can't—"). Goes quiet and short when something actually lands.
- **Says when**: Trail — the intro. Harbor — watching Cargo Row, reads it as opportunity; the night break-in. Ember — accepts Halloran's pin, happy. Signal — in Meridian colours, sees the player's Mega, wants one. Sprawl — told his mother is alive, doesn't believe it, loses, goes quiet, leaves. Underlight — "Bet you can't do this without me." No pin. Postgame — full Mega team, still talks in challenges, means them less.
- **Battles**: Trail (Lv.16), Harbor (Lv.~21), Signal (Lv.~31, sponsored), Sprawl (Lv.~37, angry), Underlight alongside the player (ally), postgame rematches (Mega).

### Elena Voss — the Warden
The field engineer who ordered the drill stopped and was blamed when it wasn't. Went down within a year of the Incident and has spent eighteen years in the Old Lines keeping Verdanyx company. Not a hermit by temperament — by duty. Precise, calm, someone who's talked to a Pokémon more than to people for most of two decades and it shows in how carefully she chooses words for humans.
- **Role**: the truth, and the ending's mechanism. Her Key Stone is the one the player carries. Her Absol is the one the player is given. Her bond with Verdanyx is what lets it revert.
- **Voice**: measured, exact, unsentimental until she asks about her son and then very quiet.
- **Says when**: Never before U3 except through the Absol and the note in the cache. U3 — everything. Act 2 close — "Bring my son, if he'll come." Underlight — "Later. Go." Ending — she comes up.

### Director Vance
Physically unremarkable on purpose. Speaks like someone who has rehearsed being reasonable, because he has. Treats disagreement as a puzzle. Keeps a resonance-scarred drill-bit fragment from the original excavation on his desk and has never explained it to anyone. Capable of genuine kindness to people he's decided are useful, and it isn't fully performance.
- **Role**: the true believer. He thinks about the people at the bottom of the stack every day; he's just decided the way to help them runs through something that doesn't get a vote.
- **Voice**: calm, precise. The composure cracks only when the excavation itself comes up.
- **Says when**: Referenced in Harbor/Ember/Greenline by others, never seen. Signal — a recorded address plays in the server farm. Tower — the conversation; "what," not "who." After losing — activates the rig anyway; "go and be right." Ending — resigns rather than lie.

### Professor Mabosso
Older than the archetype implies; tired in a way that isn't about age. On the original division young enough to believe in it, left old enough to know what it cost him. Warm about Pokémon, clipped about Meridian. Genuinely likes his work independent of the stakes.
- **Role**: the mentor who knows more than he says, and the one who finally explains deep resonance and Mega Evolution at K4.
- **Voice**: warm and rambling about Pokémon; careful and short the moment a question edges toward Meridian's history.
- **Says when**: Lab — starters. Any visit before K4 — deflects. K4 — the whole history, and that he was there. He asks the player not to tell Dario yet. After U3 — "She's alive? …I hoped. I never let myself more than hope."

### Dr. Ines Halloran — Meridian's community-relations director
The human face of the company. Funds the Scrapyard Exchange's repairs, sponsors local trainers, and singles Dario out because she genuinely thinks a Voss deserves better than the Outskirts gave him. **She doesn't know about the excavation's real purpose.** She's the proof that Meridian isn't a monolith — most of it is people doing infrastructure work.
- **Role**: the kind offer. Everything she gives Dario is meant. That's the trap and it isn't hers.
- **Voice**: warm, professional, remembers everyone's name and uses it. Never oily; that's the point.
- **Says when**: Ember — meets Dario. Signal — on the sidelines. Sprawl — the contract; then, alone, "What did you tell him?" and the access badge. Ending — her testimony is part of what surfaces.

## District casts

Each district has a shop, a gym, and four to six named people. Flavor NPCs are here too — "not plot-relevant" shouldn't mean cardboard.

### The Outskirts (Act 1)
- **Camper Reyes** — quotes outdoor-survival advice that's clearly made up on the spot.
- **Cyclist Dana** — narrates her own battles in third person like a sports broadcast.
- **Skater Milo** — can't sit still; even his dialogue boxes should feel mid-motion.
- **Busker Talia** — answers most questions with whatever song lyric feels closest. *(Later: the "Song She Can't Finish" side quest in Signal District — she's been trying to write down a melody she hears in the storms.)*
- **Courier Zeke** — perpetually late to something unrelated to the battle.
- **The Underpass mechanic (Ferro)** — works with Alma; a rematch partner once rematches exist.

### Harbor District
- **Leader Coral** (she/her) — Water. Treats every battle like a performance for the empty aquarium tanks. Post-badge: "The tanks liked that one."
- **Harbormaster Rossi** (he/him) — runs what's left of official harbor business. Controls the Ferry Terminal. Says outright that boats used to leave and stopped for reasons nobody official explains. Side quest: *The Ferry That Never Left* (drain the Drowned Stair with the old ferry pumps) → Gyaradosite. Ending: the ferry runs.
- **Maren** (she/her) — Cargo Row dockworker, off-duty at the Fish Market where she's willing to talk. Recognises the dig codes on Meridian's crates from her father's time on the original excavation. The tip that sends the player into Cargo Row.
- **Keeper Ambrose** (he/him) — the lighthouse keeper. The only structure in Zau older than the stacking, and he acts like it. Regional history that isn't corporate-sourced: "Zau wasn't always up. It was down first." Side quest: *The Keeper's Log* (find three markers from the pre-city harbor).
- **Fish Market vendor (Nadia)** — the shop. Sells fish-themed everything, opinionated about Water types.
- **Meridian Site Security (trainer class)** — polite, corporate, logo on the jacket. "This is a restricted area, and I'm going to have to ask you to leave. …After."

### Ember Quarter
- **Leader Ashgrave** (he/him) — Fire. Blunt, impatient with small talk, softens the moment the battle's actually good. Never stops the furnace. Postgame rematch → Cameruptite.
- **Foreman Kettering** (he/him) — runs the last fully staffed line at the Kilns. The district's memory. Complains, loudly, about the brownouts. E3: needs the line restarted. Side quest: *The Last Line* (restore all three mill lines) → Lucarionite.
- **The substation technician (Ozren)** — Meridian contractor. Badge-tapped and tight-lipped on site. At the Exchange, off the clock, will admit the trunk cable "goes further down than any drawing I've seen."
- **Scrapyard Exchange keeper (Bo)** — sells off pallets, till is a coffee tin. Halloran is funding his roof.
- **Two mill workers (Ila, Dev)** — trainers in the Boiler Tunnels, off shift, battling because there's nothing else to do in a blackout.
- **Dr. Ines Halloran** — first appears here (see main cast).

### Greenline Terraces
- **Leader Thistle** (they/them) — Grass. Talks to their own Pokémon mid-battle more than to the challenger. Postgame rematch → Pinsirite.
- **Old Sato** (he/him) — head gardener since the terraces were planted. Noticed the plants near the intake growing wrong; stopped planting there; stopped mentioning it. Side quest: *What Grows There* (three cuttings for Priya) → Gardevoirite.
- **The Meridian hydrologist (Dr. Kess)** — polite, precise, unable to say where the intake draws from beyond "a deep aquifer."
- **Seed Bank keeper (Wren)** — treats every purchase as a small bet on the future.
- **Allotment regulars (three trainers: Tomas, Grandma Osei, a kid called Pip)** — the *Orchard Bet* side quest → Heracronite. Grandma Osei is the strongest trainer in the district and everyone knows it but her.

### Signal District
- **Leader Prism** (they/them) — Electric. Runs Signal Tower as a lab; losing is data. S1/S2: the storm signature. "That's not weather. That's a pulse."
- **Prism's assistant (Juno)** — runs the errands with the player through the cable risers; nervous around Meridian technicians because they used to be one.
- **The server-farm shift lead (Marchetti)** — Meridian. Not unkind, not curious, wishes the player would stop asking about power draw.
- **Signal District shop (the Relay, run by twins Ash and Ren)** — finish each other's sentences, sell Electric-type gear.
- **Busker Talia** returns — she's followed the melody up here. *The Song She Can't Finish* → Alakazite.

### The Undercity
- **Leader Obsidian** (she/her) — Dark. Dry, unbothered, has seen stranger things than a trainer walking in. The one leader who touches the plot: she knows the Warden exists and won't say more until after the badge. Gives the player the Gengarite: "You'll need it down there."
- **The Warden** — Elena Voss (see main cast).
- **Tunnel dwellers (trainer class)** — people who chose the Undercity over the stack. Not villains, not victims. "You're the first one from up top who's asked instead of told."
- **Kestrel** (she/her) — runs the Vault-adjacent trading post; the Undercity's shop. Knows every rumour and sells half of them.
- **The old station master (Halvard)** — remembers the transit lines running. Marks the years in chalk. Some of the shrines are his.

### The Sprawl
- **Dr. Ines Halloran** — her community office is here.
- **Sprawl residents (trainer class: apartment kids, shop owners, a building manager)** — Meridian is their landlord, employer, and sponsor, and most of them like it fine. This is the district that makes the company *ordinary*.
- **A Meridian recruiter (Sloane)** — offers the player what Halloran offered Dario. Declining is a dialogue choice with no penalty; the player isn't for sale, and the game shouldn't pretend it's tempting.

### Meridian Tower
- **Director Vance** (see main cast).
- **Executives and Site Security (trainer classes)** — floors of them. The executives are the only trainers in the game who apologise for winning.
- **Vance's assistant (Amara)** — lets the player up. Doesn't say why. Knows.

### The Skyline (postgame)
- **The weather-station keeper (Iven)** — the last person who watched the storms form from above. Guides the player to Verdanyx at rest.
