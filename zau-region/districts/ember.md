# Ember Quarter

*Old industrial Zau, still running hot.*

One stratum up from the Harbor, the city stops spreading and starts stacking. The Ember Quarter is the first layer that was built *on purpose* — foundries, kilns, and rolling mills raised on the harbor's back when Zau still made things instead of managing them. Half of it is shuttered now. The other half runs around the clock, and the whole district breathes the heat: brick warm to the touch, air that shimmers over the grates, a permanent orange glow on the underside of the stratum above.

## Sub-locations

- **Ashgrave's Foundry Gym** — Leader Ashgrave's gym is the working floor of a blast-furnace hall. No bleachers, no announcer — you battle between the ladles while the furnace runs, and Ashgrave makes a point of never stopping the line for a challenger. Blunt, impatient with small talk, visibly softens the moment the battle is actually good (see [`CHARACTERS.md`](../CHARACTERS.md)).
- **The Scrapyard Exchange** — the district's shop, run out of a salvage yard. Everything is sold off pallets; the till is a coffee tin. Where Harbor's market sells fish, this one sells whatever the mills throw out, and the odd useful thing the workers trade in.
- **The Kilns** — a row of old brick kilns at the district's low end, most of them cold. **Foreman Kettering** runs the one factory line here that still runs fully staffed. He's the district's memory: knows who used to own what, and complains — loudly, to anyone — about the brownouts that started the month Meridian's substation went live.
- **Meridian Substation** — a new, clean, fenced power substation wedged between two century-old mills. Officially it "stabilises the Quarter's grid." The Quarter's grid has been *less* stable since it went in. *(Story hook — see below.)*
- **The Freight Lift** — the district's connection up. A caged industrial lift that used to carry finished steel to the strata above. Locked from the top side until the Greenline Terraces are built out.
- **The Harbor Ramp** — the way in and out. A wide haul-road ramp down to the Harbor's Cargo Row end, where the mills' output used to meet the boats.

## People

- **Leader Ashgrave** — already established (Fire, Ember Quarter).
- **Foreman Kettering** — runs the last fully staffed line at the Kilns. The district's institutional memory; not political, just observant. The NPC who'll tell you the brownouts are new.
- **A substation technician** — Meridian contractor, on-site, badge-tapped and tight-lipped. Won't discuss what the substation draws or where the trunk cable goes. Off-site (at the Exchange, later) might say more.

## Story hook

Harbor's hook was *cargo moving with no boats to move it*. Ember's is *power moving with nothing to power*.

Meridian's substation draws far more load than every working mill in the Quarter combined — Kettering's brownouts are the Quarter's grid being bled. The trunk cable doesn't run up toward Meridian Tower. It runs down, into a sealed old service tunnel behind the Kilns.

**Resolved in [`STORY.md`](../STORY.md), kept from the player for now:** this is the excavation's power feed. Director Vance's reopened dig under the Outskirts needs more current than the sealed tunnels ever had, and the Ember Quarter — the one stratum with heavy industrial grid capacity sitting mostly idle — is where he's quietly taking it from. Cargo Row moves the equipment; the Ember substation runs it. Two districts, two halves of the same operation, and neither one looks like anything from street level. The player shouldn't be able to put them together yet; each district's hook should read as its own local oddity until Act 3.

## Wild Pokémon

All real species, industrial-district-appropriate (Fire/Rock/Steel per [`WORLD.md`](../WORLD.md)). Suggested level band: 17–25 (post-Coral, pre-Ashgrave).

| Species | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Slugma | Fire | common — pools of them around the working furnaces |
| Numel | Fire/Ground | slow, dozing on the warm brick |
| Aron | Steel/Rock | eats the scrap; the Exchange hates them |
| Rolycoly | Rock | common — the Kilns' coal heaps are full of them |
| Litwick | Ghost/Fire | the cold kilns, after dark |
| Torkoal | Fire | uncommon, the Foreman's line has one as an unofficial mascot |
| Magnemite | Electric/Steel | clustered around the substation's fence — a small tell that something's drawing power |
| Houndour | Dark/Fire | rare — the "you got lucky" spawn, same slot as Harbor's Pelipper |

Three of these lines end in a real Mega-Evolution-eligible species — **Aggron**, **Camerupt**, **Houndoom** — which is deliberate: it grows the Mega roster the same way the Outskirts' Riolu/Gible/Absol did.

## Connections

- **Down**: the Harbor Ramp, to the Harbor District's Cargo Row end. Gated on Coral's badge — Foundry security doesn't let unbadged trainers up the ramp.
- **Up**: the Freight Lift, toward Greenline Terraces. Locked until that stratum exists.
- **Down (hidden)**: the sealed service tunnel behind the Kilns that the substation's trunk cable runs into. Not a route the player can take yet — it's where the Ember hook and the Harbor's Drowned Stair eventually converge.
