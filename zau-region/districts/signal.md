# Signal District

*The city's nervous system, bolted onto its side.*

Signal District isn't a layer so much as a growth — comms towers, server halls and antenna farms built up the outside of the stack where the wind is, because that's where the signal is. It's the loudest quiet place in Zau: everything hums, nothing talks. Meridian's data centre is the biggest tenant, and the district's whole economy is keeping it cool.

## Sub-locations

- **Signal Tower** — Leader Prism's gym, which Prism runs as a lab. The battle floor is an anechoic chamber; your footsteps don't echo and it's unsettling. Prism's storm-signature research lives here (STORY.md S1).
- **The Relay** — the district's shop, run by twins Ash and Ren, who finish each other's sentences.
- **Meridian Data Centre** — the server farm. Marchetti's shift. The place Prism and Priya pull the storm signature from (S2), with the player running the cable risers.
- **The Cable Risers** — the S2 dungeon: vertical maintenance shafts between the data centre's floors, Electric/Steel wilds, Meridian technicians as trainers.
- **The Antenna Farm** — the district's edge, where Talia ends up (side quest 6). From here the storms are *audible* before they're visible.
- **The Sprawl Bridge** — the way up, a sky bridge to the Sprawl. Opens after Badge 5 (Act 3).
- **The Service Stair** — the way down, to the Greenline. Gated on Thistle's badge from below.

## People
Leader Prism, Juno, Marchetti, Ash and Ren, Busker Talia (returning). See CHARACTERS.md.

## Story hook — *signal*
The storms have an electrical signature that matches no weather pattern. Cleaned up, it's rhythmic, slow — a pulse. Prism doesn't care about Meridian; Prism cares that the data doesn't make sense. This is the district where the "Meridian's doing something" thread becomes "something down there is *alive*."
