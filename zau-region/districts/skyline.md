# The Skyline (postgame)

*Above Meridian Tower, where the storms used to form.*

Antenna farms, sky bridges, weather stations. Opens after the ending. The Skyline is where Verdanyx goes to rest — as high above the city as it was once below it — and where the game's hardest fights live.

## Sub-locations
- **The Weather Stations** — the postgame dungeon route (SIDEQUESTS.md #10), Iven as guide.
- **The Highest Station** — Verdanyx at rest; the Garchompite.
- **The Sky Bridges** — the League's real elite as rematches, gym leaders with full Mega teams.

## Wild Pokémon (real, Flying/Electric/Dragon; band 50+)
Swablu (→ Altaria, **Mega-eligible**), Bagon (shared; → Salamence, **Mega-eligible**), Skarmory, Rotom, rare Dratini line.
