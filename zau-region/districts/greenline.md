# Greenline Terraces

*The city's one deliberate green layer.*

Above the Ember Quarter's smoke, Zau tried to apologise. The Greenline is a stack of garden terraces built up the face of the stratum — planters the size of streets, hedgerows grown over old mill roofs, glasshouses where the foundry chimneys used to vent. It was designed to look like nature reclaimed the concrete. Nature did not do this; a planning committee did, and the district's charm is that after forty years of growing it has stopped mattering which.

Everything here is irrigated from below. That is the part nobody thinks about.

## Sub-locations

- **Thistle's Canopy Gym** — Leader Thistle's gym is the top terrace's glasshouse, humid and loud with insects. Thistle talks to their own Pokémon mid-battle more than to the challenger (see [`CHARACTERS.md`](../CHARACTERS.md)) — you're not being ignored, you're just not the conversation.
- **The Seed Bank** — the district's shop, run out of the old horticultural archive. Seeds, tools, and whatever the gardeners have found useful enough to stock; the shopkeeper treats every purchase as a small bet on the future.
- **The Allotments** — the middle terrace, parcelled out to residents from the strata below who come up to grow things. Kids, trainers between gyms, retirees. Low-stakes, the district's social floor.
- **Meridian Irrigation Works** — the pump station that feeds every terrace. Meridian-built, Meridian-run, and the only thing in the Greenline that hums. The intake water comes up *warm*. *(Story hook — see below.)*
- **The Overlook** — the lower terrace's edge, where the hedges stop and the Outskirts spread out far below. **Old Sato**, the head gardener, has a bench here. From it you can watch the storms gather over the Wild Zone before anyone down there knows it's raining.
- **The Service Stair** — the way up, toward Signal District. A Meridian keycard reader at the bottom that blinks red at everyone.
- **The Freight Lift** — the way down, to the Ember Quarter. The same caged lift the mills used to send steel up; the Greenline uses it to send compost down.

## People

- **Leader Thistle** — already established (Grass, Greenline Terraces).
- **Old Sato** — head gardener since the terraces were planted. Remembers what the district looked like as bare concrete; not sentimental about it. The one who noticed the plants near the pump intake growing wrong, and the one who stopped mentioning it when nobody wanted to hear.
- **A Meridian hydrologist** — on-site at the Irrigation Works. Polite, precise, and unable to say where the intake pipe draws from beyond "a deep aquifer."

## Story hook

Harbor's hook was *cargo*. Ember's was *power*. The Greenline's is *water* — and what it's doing to what grows.

The Irrigation Works draws from far below the city, and the water comes up warm and mineral-heavy. The ferns and creepers nearest the intake are twice the size they should be, greener than the soil can account for, and Old Sato has quietly stopped planting anything there. Meridian says "aquifer." The pipe goes down a long way past any aquifer.

**Resolved in [`STORY.md`](../STORY.md), kept from the player for now:** the intake runs down into the old tunnel system, and the water carries traces of whatever the Incident released — the same thing that turned an ordinary Grass-type into Verdanyx. It's diluted enough to be harmless to people; it is not harmless to plants. This is the softest of the three district hooks on purpose: no fenced yard, no dead lift button, just gardens growing a little too well and one old man who noticed. It should read as folklore until Act 3, when the player learns what was in the water.

## Connections

- **Down**: the Freight Lift, to the Ember Quarter. Gated on Ashgrave's badge from the Ember side.
- **Up**: the Service Stair, toward Signal District. Locked until that stratum exists.
- **Down (hidden)**: the Irrigation Works' intake pipe — not a route, but the third district in a row whose Meridian installation points the same direction.
