# The Sprawl

*Where Meridian is ordinary.*

The mid-city residential and commercial layer between Signal District and the Tower — apartments, shops, schools, the district most of Zau actually lives in. Meridian is the landlord, the employer and the sponsor, and most people here like it fine. No gym. This is Act 3's first stop, and its job is to make the company *normal* right before the player walks into its head office.

## Sub-locations
- **Halloran's Community Office** — SP1/SP2. Dario's contract; Halloran's "What did you tell him?"; the access badge.
- **The Concourse** — the Sprawl's shops, including the cosmetics vendors (SIDEQUESTS.md, cosmetics).
- **The Rooftops** — Sprawl residents as trainers; a view up at the Tower and down at everything.
- **The Tower Lobby Elevator** — guarded. Opens with Halloran's badge.
- **The Sprawl Bridge** — down to Signal District.

## People
Halloran, Sloane (the recruiter), Sprawl residents. See CHARACTERS.md.
