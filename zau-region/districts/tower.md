# Meridian Tower

*Glass, sterile, and mostly full of people who don't know.*

Director Vance's HQ. Corporate floors of executives who apologise for winning and Site Security who are polite about escorting you out. The Tower is a battle already; this makes it a place.

## Sub-locations
- **The Lobby** — Site Security. Halloran's badge gets you past the desk.
- **The Floors** — T1: executives, Security, the company as infrastructure work. Amara lets the player up.
- **The Director's Office** — T2/T3. The drill-bit fragment on the desk glows faintly near the player's Key Stone. The conversation. The battle. The rig sequence from the desk.
- **The Freight Core** — V1: the elevator down through the whole stack, every district's lights flickering past.
- **The Skyline Access** — locked until postgame.

## People
Vance, Amara, Executives, Site Security. See CHARACTERS.md.

## Wild Pokémon
None — the Tower has no wilds. Every encounter is a person.
