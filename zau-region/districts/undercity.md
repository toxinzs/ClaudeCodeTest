# The Undercity

*The layer everyone built over and forgot.*

The old transit lines, sewers and service tunnels under the Outskirts — Zau before it stacked. Nobody who lives here was assigned here; they chose down over up. It's dark, quiet, older than anything above it, and the deepest tunnels are quiet in a way that is somebody's doing.

## Sub-locations

- **The Drowned Stair (top)** — the Harbor's flooded stairwell, drained in the Ferry quest (SIDEQUESTS.md #2). The way in.
- **The Old Lines** — the U1 dungeon. Transit platforms with the trains still in them, sewer junctions, the shrines. Ghost/Dark/Poison wilds; tunnel dwellers as trainers.
- **Kestrel's Post** — the trading post, the district's shop. Rumours included.
- **Undercity Vault** — Leader Obsidian's gym, a converted bank vault two levels down from the old station. The vault door is the gym door.
- **The Terminus** — *(built, Phase 28)* past the Vault: once Obsidian has handed over the Gengarite, her vault door stands open and the tunnel behind it runs down to a dead-end transit platform nobody sealed off. A 5×12 corridor like the Old Lines: two Regulars (Mireille, Ansel) at the one-tile chokepoints, a shrine and a still-lit destination board, and **Leader Halcyon** (Badge 6) on the platform. The only way out is back. Beating Halcyon hands over the Banettite and is what opens the Warden's Reach.
- **Halvard's Platform** — the old station master's home platform; the chalk years.
- **The Warden's Reach** — past the Terminus: where Elena lives. Not a dungeon; a home, in the dark, with a kettle. Empty until Halcyon's badge.
- **The Underlight (entrance)** — below the Reach. Sealed until Act 3.

## People
Leader Obsidian, Kestrel, Halvard, tunnel dwellers, the Warden (Elena Voss). See CHARACTERS.md.

## Story hook — *the truth*
No oddity here — this is where the oddities resolve. U2: Obsidian's rumours of the Warden. U3: Elena, the whole history, Verdanyx as a "who." Act 2 closes here with Vance's rig starting up.
