# Harbor District

*The one place in Zau that opens outward instead of stacking up.*

Everywhere else in the city, you're either climbing or digging. The Harbor is flat — a shelf of piers, warehouses, and flooded walkways where the stacked city finally runs out of room and hits open water. It's older than most of what's built above it, and it looks it: rust, brine, gulls nesting in gaps between shipping containers that haven't moved in years.

## Sub-locations

- **Coral's Reef Gym** — Leader Coral's gym, built into a decommissioned public aquarium. The old viewing tanks are still there, repurposed as the battle floor's back wall — you fight with a wall of fish watching you.
- **The Fish Market** — the district's actual pulse. Loud, crowded, the one place in Harbor that feels alive rather than abandoned. Vendors, a few trainers looking for casual battles, the district's item shop.
- **Cargo Row** — a fenced-off stretch of container yards flying the Meridian Dynamics logo. Nobody local works the cranes anymore; the trucks that come and go don't stop at the market. *(Story hook — see below.)*
- **The Ferry Terminal** — boarded up, "temporarily" for longer than anyone can remember. This is where water travel eventually opens up once there's a reason (and a means) to leave the harbor by boat.
- **The Boardwalk** — a promenade along the water's edge. Low-stakes, mostly wild Pokémon and kids fishing off the railing. The easiest entry point into the district.
- **Drowned Stair** — a flooded stairwell at the Boardwalk's far end, water lapping at the third step down. It doesn't obviously go anywhere yet — but it's clearly a route down toward the Undercity for whenever that connection matters.
- **Lighthouse Point** — the district's edge, a working lighthouse that's the only structure in Zau built before the stacking started. An old keeper NPC lives here with actual regional history to hand out, distinct from Meridian Dynamics' version of events.

## People

- **Leader Coral** — already established (Water, Harbor Gym).
- **Harbormaster Rossi** — runs what's left of official harbor business. Controls the Ferry Terminal. The NPC who'd tell you outright that boats used to leave from here, and stopped for reasons nobody officially explains.
- **A Cargo Row dockworker** — off-duty, at the Fish Market, not on the clock and more willing to talk there. Suspicious of what Meridian's actually shipping through a harbor that supposedly doesn't run boats anymore. Small thread pulling on the same rumor the intro cutscene already plants ("chasing rumors of something asleep beneath the city") — the cargo might not be arriving by sea at all, but heading down.
- **The lighthouse keeper** — flavor and regional lore, deliberately not corporate-sourced information.

## Story hook

Meridian Dynamics fenced off Cargo Row and the Ferry Terminal is "closed," but cargo keeps moving. If the boats aren't running, where's it actually going?

**Resolved in [`STORY.md`](../STORY.md), kept from the player for now:** it's not arriving by sea at all. Director Vance is quietly reopening the sealed excavation from eighteen years ago, and Cargo Row is one of the routes moving equipment and personnel toward it — down, via the Drowned Stair's connection to the Undercity, not out to open water. The dockworker NPC's suspicion is correct; she just doesn't have the full picture, and neither should the player yet. Harbor is a good place to plant this specific piece because it's the one district positioned to physically connect outward (water) and downward (Drowned Stair) at the same time — the perfect place for Meridian to move things without going through street-level Zau at all.

## Connections

- **Up/inland**: toward Ember Quarter, following the district progression already implied by League order.
- **Down**: Drowned Stair, not yet functional — a placeholder connection to the Undercity for whenever that stratum gets built out.
- **Out**: the Ferry Terminal, Zau's eventual water-travel gate.
