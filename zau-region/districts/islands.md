# The Islands (postgame)

*Zau wasn't always up. It was down first — and it was never the whole world.*

Not a second region. Two small, named islands, each with a clear reason to exist rather than being a fully populated town — the region's true endgame, reached only once the player has all 8 badges and Harbormaster Rossi's ferry actually runs (the real payoff of *The Ferry That Never Left*, SIDEQUESTS.md #2, alongside its Gyaradosite). The first time anyone in this story leaves the stack sideways instead of climbing or dropping through it, which mirrors Verdanyx's own arc — contained underneath, freed to rest at the top — with the player's: contained in the stack, free to leave it. Neither island gates the other, and neither gates the Skyline; a postgame save can pursue all three in any order.

Each island anchors one of the region's final two gym leaders. That's deliberate — these aren't optional filler on top of an already-finished game, they're where badges 7 and 8 live.

---

## The Long Shoal — Leader Fathom, Water/Dark

Wide-open, non-vertical terrain: the one thing Zau's geography structurally cannot have. No docks stacked on docks, no district built on the last one's roof — just water, sky, and a shoreline. Real ocean-coded Pokémon that don't fit any of Zau's urban/industrial wild tables, Harbor's dockside roster included.

### Sub-locations
- **The Landing** — where the ferry actually puts in. A handful of people who fish, not commute.
- **The Shoal** — the open water itself, where Fathom fights: no building, no sponsor, a boat and a long sandbar.
- **The Wreck Line** — the shallows where old hulls (older than Zau's stack, older than the Ferry Terminal ever was) show at low tide. Flavor for now; a natural fishing/diving-mechanic hook later.

### Leader Fathom (they/them)
Talks like someone who's spent more time reading weather off the water than off a screen. Unhurried about winning or losing the same way the tide is unhurried — "Sea doesn't care how many of these you've got." Ace: **Mega Sharpedo**, holding the **Sharpedonite**, handed over with the badge itself (see MEGA.md).

### Wild Pokémon (real, Water/Dark; band 60–65)
Wingull and Tentacool (shared with Harbor, higher band here), Carvanha (→ Sharpedo), Wailmer, Clamperl, rare Huntail/Gorebyss line.

## The Drowned Archive — Leader Cairn, Psychic/Steel

Ruins that predate Zau itself — the physical answer, never fully spoken aloud in the main story, to Ambrose's "it was down first." Half-sunk stonework and old machinery nobody built the stack to remember, home to the region's one true rarity outside Verdanyx.

### Sub-locations
- **The Cairn Stair** — the climb up from the landing, switchbacked stone predating any Meridian construction technique.
- **The Archive** — the ruin's inner chamber. Cairn's gym: no gym building, just the ruin itself, artifacts left exactly where they were found rather than displayed.
- **The Latias/Latios Roost** *(placement TBD — pick one, not both)* — a single, real, catchable encounter. Guarded rather than owned by the ruins; it chooses to stay, "same as some other things do" (Cairn's line, a deliberate echo of Elena and Verdanyx without naming either).

### Leader Cairn (he/him)
Precise, a little formal, treats old things with the same care Elena treats Verdanyx. Talks about the ruins more than himself until after the badge. Ace: **Mega Metagross**, holding the **Metagrossite**, handed over with the badge itself (see MEGA.md).

### Wild Pokémon (real, Psychic/Steel/ruins-coded; band 63–68)
Baltoy (→ Claydol), Beldum (→ Metang → Metagross, **Mega-eligible**), Solrock, Lunatone, rare Duskull/Dusclops line (shared flavor with the Terminus, older tenants of the same pre-city dark).

## Open questions for whenever these get built
- Exact island count could grow past two later (SIDEQUESTS.md's own expansion doc floated 2–4); these two are the load-bearing pair because they carry badges 7–8. A third/fourth island stays a pure exploration add, not required.
- Latios vs. Latias for the Archive's single encounter — either is real and correct; picking one is a coin flip, not a design decision.
- How the player actually travels between the mainland, the Long Shoal, and the Drowned Archive (one ferry with stops vs. two separate short hops) is an implementation detail for whenever this district gets built, not a bible-level decision.
